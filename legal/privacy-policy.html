1. Introduction

Aero Intelli is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning the collection, use, maintenance, and protection of personal data across our organic aeroponic growth platforms, SaaS agricultural management dashboard, and digital websites.

By accessing or using our websites, software services, or physical smart agricultural monitoring products, you consent to the data collection and processing methods described herein.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of data depending on how you interact with our technology services:

  • Personal Information: Name, professional email address, billing address, contact phone numbers, and payment details.
  • Farm & Telemetry Data: Real-time environmental metrics from your aeroponic chambers, system flow rates, crop yields, pH levels, and software telemetry logs.
  • Usage Data: Device IP address, operating system, browser configuration, page views, and operational duration on our SaaS agriculture platform.

3. How We Use Information

The data we capture serves to power, sustain, and improve your farming operations. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Operate, calibrate, and upgrade your smart aeroponic monitoring hardware and software systems.
  • Perform diagnostic checks and prevent software anomalies or hardware system failures.
  • Generate performance benchmarks, environmental insights, and crop health metrics on your dashboard.
  • Process transactional payments and handle customer support inquiries.
  • Optimize the efficiency and resource sustainability of our global smart irrigation networks.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, tokens, and web beacons to recognize your account status and keep your configuration active between browser sessions.

These tracking technologies store layout preferences, help monitor traffic metrics, and improve our security systems. You can manage or disable cookie configurations within your local browser preferences; however, disabling them may limit the operational capacity of our SaaS dashboard.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Aero Intelli does not sell or distribute personal data or proprietary farm logs to commercial advertising entities.

We only share information with trusted third-party providers (such as secure cloud storage nodes, payment gateways, and backend analytical tools) who operate under strict non-disclosure obligations to help deliver our platforms.

Additionally, we may disclose records if requested by statutory authorities to comply with safety compliance, standard legal audits, or state regulations.

6. Data Security

We deploy robust administrative, operational, and digital security measures designed to guard customer records. This includes AES encryption at rest, secure SSL/TLS protocols during data transit, and restricted firewall access logs.

While we apply maximum professional caution, no web system can guarantee 100% absolute safety. We maintain automated system checks and database backups to minimize risks and maintain integrity.

7. Data Retention

Aero Intelli holds your personal information and agricultural logs only as long as you maintain an active dashboard account, or as required by regulatory compliance rules.

Once your subscription terminates, we systematically purge personal identifier links. Telemetry data is completely anonymized and integrated into long-term agricultural performance research logs to improve our yield optimization algorithms.

8. User Rights

Depending on your state or regional location, you are entitled to certain privacy rights, including:

  • The right to inspect what personal databases are compiled under your name.
  • The right to correct or update obsolete data attributes.
  • The right to request complete deletion of personal records.
  • The right to limit processing or restrict transfers of your telemetry logs.

To initiate a privacy request, please submit a query to our support email listed below.

9. Children's Privacy

Our software systems, agritech devices, and customer support channels are intended solely for professional business operations and users who are at least 18 years of age.

We do not knowingly store or collect attributes related to children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal details, please contact us immediately to purge the record.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to, and processed in, server infrastructure located outside your immediate state or country. We utilize standard contractual protection agreements and secure data pipelines to safeguard your records across international boundaries.

11. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify or adjust this Privacy Policy when updating our systems or responding to security regulations. When amendments occur, we will update the "Last Updated" timestamp at the top of this document.

We suggest checking this page periodically to remain informed about our storage safety policies.
