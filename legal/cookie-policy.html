1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember user preferences, improve user experiences, and provide analytical insights.

Cookies do not typically contain personally identifiable information, but information collected through cookies may be associated with other information you provide to us.

2. How We Use Cookies

By continuing to use our website, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with this Cookie Policy unless you disable them through your browser settings or cookie preferences.

Aero Intelli uses cookies to:

  • Ensure website functionality and security.
  • Remember user preferences and settings.
  • Improve website performance.
  • Analyze website traffic and visitor behavior.
  • Enhance user experience.
  • Support marketing and communication activities.
  • Measure website effectiveness and performance.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the operation of the website and cannot be disabled in our systems. They help enable:

  • Secure navigation
  • Form submissions
  • Session management
  • Website security features
  • Authentication functions

Without these cookies, certain parts of the website may not function properly.

Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website. They may collect information such as:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on pages
  • Traffic sources
  • Device information
  • Website performance metrics

The information collected is generally aggregated and used to improve website performance.

Functional Cookies

These cookies allow the website to remember preferences and provide enhanced functionality. Examples include:

  • Language preferences
  • Region settings
  • User interface preferences
  • Saved form information

Marketing and Advertising Cookies

These cookies may be used to deliver relevant content, measure campaign effectiveness, and understand visitor interests. These cookies may be placed by Aero Intelli or approved third-party service providers.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Certain third-party services integrated into our website may place cookies on your device. Examples may include:

  • Analytics providers
  • Payment gateways
  • Video platforms
  • Social media integrations
  • Customer support tools
  • Marketing and advertising platforms

Aero Intelli does not control third-party cookies and encourages users to review the privacy policies of those providers.

5. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow users to manage, disable, or delete cookies through browser settings. You may choose to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Block certain cookies
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Receive notifications when cookies are used

Please note that disabling certain cookies may impact website functionality and user experience. You can also change your preferences on our website at any time by clicking the Cookie Settings option in our cookie banner or footer.

6. Data Protection

Information collected through cookies is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws.

We implement reasonable security measures to protect information collected through our website.
