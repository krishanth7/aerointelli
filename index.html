Frequently Asked Questions

Platform & Technology Intelligence

Essential insights into Aero Intelli's zero-chemical vertical aeroponics, smart RAS aquaculture, and AI analytics.

What is AeroIntelli?

Direct Answer

AeroIntelli is a zero-chemical vertical farming platform combining organic aeroponics, recirculating aquaculture, and AI crop intelligence for 100% certified organic production. It is launching Q4 2026.

How does AeroIntelli grow crops without pesticides?

Direct Answer

AeroIntelli uses advanced soilless growth towers that mist OMRI-listed organic nutrients directly to root structures, eliminating synthetic treatments and soil-based limitations entirely.

What is recirculating aquaculture in AeroIntelli's system?

Direct Answer

It is a closed-loop aquaculture system that maintains ecosystem dynamics with zero chemical discharge or agricultural run-off, working alongside the aeroponic towers.

How does AI factor into AeroIntelli's platform?

Direct Answer

AeroIntelli uses continuous computer-vision analysis of plant leaf development and chemical integrity to optimize flavor, trace yields, and detect anomalies early before crop loss.

Who is AeroIntelli built for?

Direct Answer

AeroIntelli is built for organic farmers, vertical farming operators, agribusinesses seeking certification-ready production, and sustainability-focused food producers wanting to eliminate synthetic pesticides and chemical inputs entirely.

When is AeroIntelli launching and how do I get access?

Direct Answer

AeroIntelli is launching Q4 2026. You can request access and join the waitlist of over 2,400 innovators directly on the AeroIntelli homepage at https://aerointelli.com/.
