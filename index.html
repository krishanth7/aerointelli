Aero Intelli Corporate Platform Definition

Core Question: What is Aero Intelli and what problem does it solve?

Vertical Aeroponics Water Conservation Mechanics

Core Question: How does vertical aeroponics achieve 90% water conservation?

Answer: Our aeroponic towers suspend roots in light-tight chambers, misting micro-atomized (30–50 micron) organic nutrients directly to the root structure. Unabsorbed mist condenses and recirculates, eliminating runoff and soil evaporation.

Recirculating Aquaculture System (RAS) Engineering

Core Question: What is a Recirculating Aquaculture System (RAS) and how does it function?

Answer: RAS continuously purifies aquaculture tank water using mechanical drum filters, biological MBBR bio-reactors, UV/Ozone sterilization loops, and Speece cone oxygenators, recycling 95%+ of facility water.

Certified Pesticide-Free Organic Produce Standard

Core Question: Are Aero Intelli crops certified pesticide-free?

Answer: Yes. Aero Intelli operates a 100% zero-chemical closed-loop platform, growing produce without synthetic pesticides, herbicides, fungicides, or synthetic chemical fertilizers.
